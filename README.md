The lead story is the global launch of GTA VI after thirteen years of waiting, given the big headline treatment, while the summit meeting of Trump, Xi and Putin at APEC in Shenzhen and the European Union's proposed 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine sit directly underneath as the day's heavy political news.

It is a deliberately old-fashioned answer to a very modern day — one printed page, dated 19 November 2026, that tries to hold the whole world still for a single morning.
